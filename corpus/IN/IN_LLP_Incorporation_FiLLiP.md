# LLP Incorporation via FiLLiP and Annual LLP Compliance

## Structural requirements {phase="Pre-Incorporation" clause="LLP Act 2008 s.6, s.7"}

| Requirement | Rule | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum partners | 2 (individuals or body corporates) | Section 6 |
| Designated partners | At least 2, who must be individuals | Section 7 |
| Resident designated partner | At least one designated partner must be resident in India (stayed at least 120 days in the financial year) | Section 7(1) as amended |
| Capital | No minimum contribution | — |
| Liability | Limited to agreed contribution | Section 26 |

## Name reservation — RUN-LLP {phase="Pre-Incorporation" clause="RUN-LLP"}

The proposed name can be reserved using the RUN-LLP (Reserve Unique Name) web service on the MCA portal, or as part of the FiLLiP form itself. The name must end with "LLP" or "Limited Liability Partnership" and must not resemble an existing company, LLP or registered trademark.

## Incorporation — FiLLiP {clause="FiLLiP (Form for incorporation of LLP)"}

FiLLiP is filed with the Registrar and captures the LLP's name, registered office, partners, designated partners and their contribution. It can allot a Designated Partner Identification Number (DPIN/DIN) to up to two designated partners who do not already have one. Each designated partner signs with a Class 3 DSC. On approval the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation with the LLP Identification Number (LLPIN).

## LLP Agreement — Form 3 {phase="Post-Incorporation" clause="Form 3"}

The LLP agreement, which records partners' rights, duties, profit sharing and contribution, must be filed in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. If no agreement is filed, the default provisions in the First Schedule of the LLP Act apply.

## Annual compliance {phase="Ongoing Compliance" clause="Form 8, Form 11"}

| Filing | Content | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Form 11 | Annual return (partners, contribution) | Within 60 days of the close of the financial year (30 May) |
| Form 8 | Statement of Account and Solvency | Within 30 days from the end of six months of the financial year (30 October) |
| Income tax return | ITR-5 | As notified under the Income-tax law |
| Audit | Mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh | Rule 24(8), LLP Rules 2009 |

Late filing of Form 8 or Form 11 attracts an additional fee of ₹100 per day of delay per form.
