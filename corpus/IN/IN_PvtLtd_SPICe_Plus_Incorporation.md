# Private Limited Company Incorporation via SPICe+ (INC-32)

## Overview {phase="Pre-Incorporation"}

SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus, form INC-32) is the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) web form used to incorporate a company in India under the Companies Act, 2013. It is filed on the MCA V3 portal and bundles name reservation, incorporation, and several government registrations into one integrated workflow.

SPICe+ has two parts. Part A is used for name reservation only. Part B covers incorporation itself together with the integrated registrations. Founders may file Part A first and Part B later, or file both together.

## Statutory requirements for a Private Limited Company {phase="Pre-Incorporation" clause="Companies Act 2013 s.2(68), s.149"}

| Requirement | Private Limited Company | One Person Company (OPC) | Statutory reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum directors | 2 | 1 | Section 149(1) |
| Maximum directors | 15 (more by special resolution) | 15 | Section 149(1) |
| Minimum members | 2 | 1 (natural person, Indian citizen) | Section 3(1) |
| Maximum members | 200 | 1 | Section 2(68) |
| Resident director | At least one director must have stayed in India for at least 182 days in the financial year | Same | Section 149(3) |
| Minimum paid-up capital | None (requirement removed in 2015) | None | Section 2(68) as amended |
| Nominee | Not applicable | Mandatory nominee for the sole member (Form INC-3) | Section 3(1)(c) |

## Step 1 — Digital Signature Certificates {phase="Pre-Incorporation" clause="DSC requirement"}

Every proposed director and every subscriber to the Memorandum must hold a valid Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) issued by a licensed Certifying Authority. The DSC is used to sign SPICe+ Part B and the linked e-forms. Foreign nationals must obtain a DSC with apostilled or consularised identity and address proof.

## Step 2 — SPICe+ Part A: name reservation {phase="Pre-Incorporation" clause="SPICe+ Part A"}

- Run the proposed name through the MCA name-availability check and the trademark registry search before filing.
- Up to two proposed names can be submitted in Part A, in order of preference.
- The name must comply with Section 4 of the Companies Act and the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014 (Rule 8). It must not be identical or too similar to an existing company, LLP or registered trademark, and must end with "Private Limited" (or "(OPC) Private Limited").
- If the Registrar (Central Registration Centre, CRC) marks the application for resubmission, it can be resubmitted once.
- A name approved through Part A for a new company is reserved for 20 days from the date of approval. Part B must be filed within that window or the name lapses.

## Step 3 — SPICe+ Part B: incorporation {phase="Incorporation" clause="SPICe+ Part B"}

Part B captures the company's capital structure, registered office, subscribers, directors and the declarations required for incorporation. It also integrates the following services, so separate applications are not needed:

- Allotment of Director Identification Number (DIN) for up to three proposed directors who do not already hold one.
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) for the company.
- Registration with EPFO (provident fund) and ESIC (employee state insurance).
- Profession Tax registration where the state is integrated (for example Maharashtra, Karnataka and West Bengal).
- Opening of a company bank account through the integrated bank list.
- GSTIN application (optional at this stage).

## Step 4 — Linked e-forms filed with Part B {phase="Incorporation" clause="SPICe+ linked forms"}

| Linked form | Purpose | Who signs |
|---|---|---|
| e-MoA (INC-33) | Memorandum of Association: name, registered state, objects, liability, capital clause, subscribers | All subscribers (DSC) |
| e-AoA (INC-34) | Articles of Association: internal rules (share transfer restrictions, board powers, meetings) | All subscribers (DSC) |
| AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35) | Applications for GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, Profession Tax and bank account | Director (DSC) |
| INC-9 | Declaration by subscribers and first directors (auto-generated from Part B) | Subscribers and directors (DSC) |
| URC-1 | Only when an existing firm or LLP converts into a company | Applicant |

Companies with special articles (for example, investor-protection clauses) may attach physical MoA/AoA instead of e-MoA/e-AoA where permitted by the form.

## Step 5 — Documents to keep ready {phase="Incorporation" clause="SPICe+ attachments"}

- Identity proof (PAN for Indian nationals; passport for foreign nationals) and address proof (bank statement or utility bill not older than two months) for every director and subscriber.
- Proof of registered office: a recent utility bill (not older than two months) plus a rent agreement and No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the owner if the premises are not owned.
- Passport-size photographs of directors and subscribers.
- Consent to act as director (DIR-2) and declaration of non-disqualification, where required.

## Step 6 — Fees, stamp duty and approval {phase="Incorporation" clause="SPICe+ fees"}

MCA does not charge a registration fee for SPICe+ when the company's authorised capital is up to ₹15 lakh. Above that, fees follow the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014. Stamp duty on the MoA and AoA is payable separately and varies by state; it is calculated automatically on the portal.

On approval the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation (COI) carrying the Corporate Identity Number (CIN), along with the company PAN and TAN. The DINs of the new directors are allotted at the same time.

## Common reasons for resubmission {phase="Incorporation" clause="SPICe+ resubmission"}

- The objects clause in the e-MoA is vague or does not match the proposed name.
- The registered-office utility bill is older than two months or the NOC is missing.
- Mismatches between names on PAN, DSC and the form.
- The proposed name resembles an existing trademark.
